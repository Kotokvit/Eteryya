He became a magician because he wanted to accumulate knowledge and become an ultimate existence. He did many experiments and hurt others in the pursuit of his goals, and eventually became a Legendary Great Magician. [44] Skills[ ] Braham’s unique characteristic is to use the magic of the lowest level several times, up to 20 times [45], simultaneously [46] with an unbelievable high attack power. [47] There is no delay between his spells [48] due to the multi-spellcasting that has been achieved as a result of combining the master level of spells and a passive skill. [49] It requires an incredible computing ability, even if they are the lowest rated magic. With his own unique magic style, Braham is well-rounded and specialized in one on one matches. [50] Braham is someone who dominates the battlefield by predicting the enemy’s path and laying traps. [51] The Alarm spell is used to precisely match the timing. He anticipates the opponent’s action in real time during a battle, sets the time for the alarm spell, as well as the attack magic trajectory. [52] Braham is able to coordinate his magic in real time depending on the situation, what is perceived as the enemy. He has complete control over his magic. The so-called great magicians are obsessed with the formula, while Braham’s magic changes shape as needed. [53] Being a great magician and having a lot of mana in his past, he had always been accustomed of having enough mana. He never learnt to get along with little mana like everyone else. Therefore, during his first experience with Grid, he made the mistake of not controlling his mana properly. But it can also be seen as the reason why his magic consumes a considerable amount of mana. [54] Appearance[ ] Braham’s transcendent beauty as a vampire is impossible even for a brilliant sculptor to carve completely. It is so great that some people in the world thought of it as the ideal appearance. [55] Whenever females see him, they scream or lose their souls. The same is true for some males. It is not only humans but also other races as well to a certain level. They all react equally to him. [56] He has eyes like the Red Phoenix’s Breath. [57] Personality[ ] In later years, Braham started to feel human emotions and is craving for affection. [58] But Braham awakened emotions without knowing it. [59] His personality changed drastically after losing eternal life and living as a human. As an imperfect existence, he feels a conflict before his instincts of obsession for knowledge and the humanity he acquired. [60] Braham has the idea that he should reign as the supreme being on Earth and the pride is too high to speak his heart. [61] Furthermore, he looks down on others, including his own kin. It makes him arrogant [62], causing him to find it hard to respect and associate with others, which leaves him lonely. Living a life where he lies to himself and others, turning a blind eye to his changing self. His hard-to-judge personality [63] seems to suggest he has somewhat of a multiple personality disorder. [64] The passionate Braham has a lot of knowledge and experience but he often lets his emotions get ahead of his reason. It is extremely rare for anyone to say that Duke of Wisdom Braham is wise. Braham enjoys trampling one-sidedly against his enemy instead of fighting them. [65] He can barely suppress his anger after being mocked and will attempt to run wild. [66] He was jealous of Mumud and could not tolerate his disciple, who has more talent than himself, only to feel guilty shortly afterwards that he was jealous and ruined his life. [67] It was Braham in a half-crazy state who infiltrated Trauka’s nest and was lucky enough to have succeeded in stealing Amon’s Book after almost dying several times. [68] He was originally very strong in spirit and his basic tendencies were cruel. But ironically, he is weak towards those he likes. [69] His popularity for his appearance, in which he experiences many serious things, annoys him. [70] His mouth has luxurious tastes. [71] Relationships[ ] Braham hates and miss Pagma, his formerly only friend who amazed and betrayed him. He tried to kill Pagma’s Descendant Grid, but also feels more and more favorable towards him. [72] The extent goes so far that Braham regards Grid as his only friend [73] and after the resurrection considers everyone as insignificant apart from Grid and ‘his family’. [74] He miss Mumud, the only person in the world who trusted and followed him. Braham wants to make up for the sins he committed, [75] feeling regretful towards his undead disciple. He envied him but never harmed him, despite intercepting his achievements. [76] Against Marie Rose who sucked his blood, he harbors a deep resentment and blames her for his mother’s death and his lost eternal life. For God Yatan who expelled and cursed his mother Braham feels a great sense of betrayal. [77] Researchs[ ] Resurrection Magic [78] - thanks to Pagma, Braham was able to study the magic of eternal life more deeply and succeeded in designing the Vessel of the Soul. Immortality Magic [79] - Braham had to regain his eternal life as a vampire and started to explore it. But he couldn’t complete the magic of eternal life. Mumud’s Magic System [80] - Braham has taken away the achievement of his disciple Mumud. Related Quests[ ] Great Magician’s Resurrection Legendary Great Magician Production Training Braham and Mumud The Soul of a Great Magician Who Lost his Power Braham’s Request History[ ] On his path to acquire knowledge, he did experiments on many creatures. Since he targeted other vampires as well, his direct descendant powers were taken and he was ejected from the clan. He disguised himself as a human and continued studying magic, while also trying to find a way to regain his immortality. [81] He once had a talented disciple named Mumud. This disciple of his spread great knowledge to the world, but somehow Mumud was forgotten about and these achievements were attributed to Braham. Due to this, Braham gained great respect from all magicians, to the extent most people overlooked him being a demonkin. [82] At another point in his life, Braham was close friends with Pagma. The two worked together on several projects, including the creation of Pavranium. Despite their close relationship, Braham was betrayed and had his life force stolen, since his friend didn’t like that he as a demonkin. [83] Braham’s body was sealed in the Sword Grave and his soul was forced to wander for hundreds of years. [84] Story[ ] Chapter 711[ ] Braham blamed himself for being out of control every time he met Lich Mumud. He left Grid’s body before he could recover his magic power. He claimed that he has recovered and wants to restore his complete body as soon as possible. This resulted in his soul scattering. Chapter 938[ ] Braham tried to recover his body which was sealed in the Sword Grave by Pagma. He was well aware that he couldn’t recover his body when he left Grid. Now Grid found him and was convinced to use Grid, once again, as his vessel. Braham entered a weakened state so assimilation was temporarily sealed. Chapter 1027-1028[ ] Due to Grid experiencing a crisis, Braham forcefully uses Assimilation. He uses a lot of power to imbue magic in Grid’s swordsmanship, then falls into a deep sleep. Notes[ ] Braham’s title Possessor of Great Knowledge given in the chapters 307 and 662 is identical to the title Duke of Wisdom given in the chapters 1028 and 1134. The descriptions of both titles are the same. Braham is a tenth child of Beriache, but not the tenth child. He already lived before Elfin Stone [85], Fenrir [86], and Marie Rose. The latter is the tenth child and last child of Beriache. [87] Braham Eshwald is the only Direct Descendant Vampire with a name of two words. Even in the original korean novel, his name is made up of two words in the status windows. Where Eshwald comes from is not explained. Note, in the translation of the names of two other vampires, one word became two words. Though Braham is said to be an Absolute in 1853, it was later said in 1896 that he is not one. It is directly stated he became one in 1981.
